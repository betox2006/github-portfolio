Steve Jobs

The man who changed technology forever

Timeline & Achievements

  • 1955: Born on February 24 in San Francisco, California.
  • 1972: Graduates from high school and enrolls in Reed College, but drops out after one semester.
  • 1976: Co-founds Apple Computer, Inc. along with Steve Wozniak and Ronald Wayne. They launch the Apple I.
  • 1977: Introduces the Apple II, which becomes a major success and helps popularize personal computers.
  • 1984: Launches the Macintosh, the first personal computer with a graphical user interface and mouse.
  • 1985: Leaves Apple after a power struggle. He founds NeXT Inc., a computer company.
  • 1996: Apple acquires NeXT, bringing Jobs back to the company.
  • 2001: Launches the iPod, revolutionizing the way we listen to music.
  • 2007: Introduces the iPhone, forever changing the world of mobile phones.
  • 2010: Launches the iPad, creating a new category of devices.
  • 2011: Resigns as CEO of Apple in August due to health issues and passes away on October 5.
